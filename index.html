Leyla's Letters — Privacy Policy

Hamle Studio · Applies to the Leyla's Letters app on Google Play and the App Store · Last updated: 6 November 2026 · Türkçe · Support

Who is responsible?

Leyla's Letters is published by Hamle Studio. For any privacy question or request: shovegame.app@gmail.com

Ads and purchases

This version has no ads and no in-app purchases. The advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / Apple IDFA) is not used, and there is no tracking across apps.

Summary

The game sends nothing without your consent. On first launch you are asked two separate questions: gameplay statistics and error reports. Both start switched off and you can decline both; no feature is disabled. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Save & privacy → Data choices. The game does not require an account and works offline.

What is collected?

ChoiceWhat is sentWhat is never sent
Gameplay statisticsApp opens and session length; level, city and puzzle results; in-game coin/gem changes (totals); quest blockers; save health (recovery, write errors); game version, language.Name, e-mail, phone, precise location, advertising ID, contacts. No click recording, no screen recording, no cookies.
Error reportsTechnical details of a game error (error message, code line), operating system and app environment version, load time.Contents of your save, personal information.

Both use only a randomly generated anonymous ID (install_id). You can see and reset it on the Data choices screen. The ID stays on your device and is not included in save codes, so sharing a save code never shares your ID.

Why?

To find where the game is too hard, where players get stuck, and to fix errors. Data is not used for advertising or profiling and is never sold.

Who processes it, and where?

ServicePurposeLocation
PostHogGameplay statisticsUnited States (us.posthog.com)
SentryError reportsUnited States (sentry.io)

Data is transferred to servers outside your country. IP addresses are not stored on the analytics side (PostHog "Discard client IP data" is on) and location estimation (GeoIP) is off. Fonts and game files ship inside the app; no third-party font requests are made.

Retention: gameplay statistics are kept for 1 year (PostHog free plan); error reports for 30 or 90 days depending on the Sentry plan, then deleted. If this changes, this page will be updated.

Your rights

To have your data deleted, send your anonymous ID (shown in Settings) to shovegame.app@gmail.com. Because nothing else identifies you, the request is handled by that ID: all statistics for it are deleted; error reports expire on their own or are deleted earlier on request. To withdraw consent, switch both choices off in Settings → Data choices; nothing is sent from then on.

Age

Leyla's Letters is designed for adults. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Your save

Your save stays on your device (the app also keeps a backup in its own folder on the device). It is never sent to a server unless you share your save code yourself. Deleting the app deletes the save on the device.

Veriler yurt dışındaki sunuculara aktarılır. IP adresleri istatistik tarafında saklanmaz (PostHog "Discard client IP data" açık); konum tahmini (GeoIP) kapalıdır. Yazı tipleri ve oyun dosyaları uygulamanın içinde gelir; üçüncü taraf kaynaklara yazı tipi isteği yapılmaz.

Hakların

Verilerinin silinmesini istersen anonim kimliğini (Ayarlar'da görünür) shovegame.app@gmail.com adresine ilet. Kimlik dışında seni tanımlayan bir bilgi tutulmadığı için talep bu kimlik üzerinden karşılanır: bu kimliğe ait tüm istatistikler silinir; hata raporları saklama süresi dolunca kendiliğinden ya da istersen daha önce silinir. Onayını geri çekmek için Ayarlar → Veri tercihleri'nde iki seçimi kapatman yeterli; o andan sonra hiçbir veri gönderilmez.

Yaş

Leyla's Letters yetişkinler için tasarlanmıştır; çocuklara yönelik değildir ve 13 yaşından küçüklerden bilerek veri toplamaz.
