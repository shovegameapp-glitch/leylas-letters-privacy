Common questions

Does the game need the internet? No. It works fully offline.

Where is my progress stored? Only on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress. To move to another device, copy your save code from Settings and load it on the new device.

Are there ads or purchases? No, this version has neither.

Privacy: nothing is sent without your consent. See the privacy policy.
